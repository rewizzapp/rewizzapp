What the app does

Curriculum first. Folder second. Teacher approval always.

The product flow is deliberately controlled. Rewizz builds a subject workspace from the curriculum, checks lesson material against that workspace, proposes candidate revision content and keeps the teacher as the final decision-maker before export.

Start with curriculum

Import the subject curriculum so Rewizz can understand terms, topics, sub-topics and learning objectives before it reads lesson folders.

Reason: prevents generic pack generation outside the taught scope.
Scan approved folders

The teacher selects folders or files. Rewizz processes supported lesson formats, detects duplicates and separates likely wrong-subject material.

No silent disk crawling. No full Drive scan.
Build candidate groups

For selected topics, Rewizz proposes restrained slide groups with source traceability, rather than dumping every raw slide into a pack.

Teacher selects what enters the pack.
Edit and export

Teachers can edit text, reorder slides, add questions, attach links and export local formats such as PDF, PowerPoint, Word, HTML and answer keys.

Google Docs and Slides are explicit online exports only.

About Rewizz

An offline-first revision-pack builder for busy teachers.

Rewizz is being built as a teacher productivity app for Windows, macOS and Chromebook PWA use. The design principle is simple: software can sort, index and prepare suggestions, but the teacher remains responsible for scope, selection, edits and final use.

Core product position

Rewizz turns existing curriculum and lesson material into teacher-reviewed revision packs. It supports local processing by default, source traceability and explicit export choices.

  • Designed for curriculum-aligned revision preparation.
  • Built to handle duplicates, recap slides and mixed folder quality.
  • Exports are intended to remain editable and portable.

Current beta boundaries

The beta is for validation and controlled testing, not a general availability release. Feature performance and platform coverage are being tested with teachers and schools.

  • No claim of full public release readiness.
  • No fake school logos, testimonials or adoption numbers.
  • No automated publishing without teacher review.

FAQ and data protection

Privacy and Data Protection is an integral part of our product.

How does Rewizz support UAE confidentiality and data-protection obligations?

Rewizz is designed around data minimisation, purpose limitation and local processing principles aligned with the UAE Personal Data Protection Law framework, including Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021. The beta does not present itself as a formal legal certification.

What teaching data does Rewizz handle?

When a teacher chooses files or folders, the app may process curriculum files, lesson slides, worksheets, extracted text, images, links, topic maps, embeddings, local preferences, pack drafts and export jobs inside the selected workspace.

What is not uploaded or retained by Rewizz by default?
  • Lesson content is not uploaded to vendor servers by default.
  • Student data, prompts, extracted lesson text and local memory are not retained in the vendor cloud by default.
  • Wrong-subject or unapproved material is not allowed into memory, embeddings or generation unless the teacher overrides it.
  • Payment card details are not stored by the app and should remain with the payment provider.
Does Rewizz require student accounts, marks or behavioural data?

No. V1 is scoped as a teacher productivity tool. It does not require student logins, student analytics, automated grading, behaviour tracking, parent portals or LMS-style student records.

Can school administrators see teacher lesson folders?

No. In the intended school licence model, administrators manage seats, licence status and account access. They do not receive access to teacher files, local workspaces, local memory or draft packs.

Does Rewizz scan Google Drive?

No broad Drive scan is planned for V1. Google access is limited to teacher-initiated Google Docs or Google Slides export using narrow permissions. Google export requires internet sign-in and should be optional.

How are local files protected?

The app is designed to read only user-approved files or folders. Sensitive tokens and selected metadata should use local secret storage or encryption, depending on platform. Source files remain unchanged unless the teacher deliberately exports or saves a new output.

Can a teacher delete local Rewizz data?

The product design requires workspace and memory deletion controls. Deleting a Rewizz workspace should remove derived indexes, topic maps and local memory for that workspace without deleting the teacher's original lesson files.

What should beta testers avoid uploading or sharing?

Beta testers should avoid sharing unnecessary student personal data, confidential school records or third-party licensed content outside their permitted use. Rewizz should be tested with permissioned teaching material and with school approval where applicable.

Where can I read more about the legal framework?

For public reference, see the UAE Government portal on data protection laws.
